Fostering Health and Connection

The FitGB Community Sports and Wellness Festival is more than just a day of sport; it is a comprehensive platform for holistic wellbeing. We provide a safe, accessible space where families and young people can experience the transformative power of physical activity, mental health awareness, and meaningful social connection. Our mission is to bridge the gap for underserved communities in London, ensuring that everyone has the opportunity to thrive.

University of Greenwich Campus

We have chosen the University of Greenwich for its safe, accessible, and well-connected environment. This campus provides a perfect setting for families and large community gatherings, offering a space where everyone can participate in sport and wellness activities.

Eco-Friendly Practices

We prioritize environmental responsibility through zero-waste initiatives, composting stations, and reusable materials across all festival activities.

Universal Accessibility

Our facilities are fully inclusive, featuring ramps, tactile paving, and sensory-friendly spaces to ensure a safe and welcoming environment for everyone.

Safe Community Space

We foster a secure atmosphere that encourages social connection and mental wellbeing, promoting diversity and youth participation in every session.
