Empowering Communities Through Sport and Wellbeing

FitGB is a UK non-profit dedicated to promoting active lifestyles and inclusive community wellbeing. We believe that sport and wellness are powerful tools for social connection and personal growth. Our mission is to create accessible, high-impact events that engage underserved communities and foster a culture of health for everyone in London.
